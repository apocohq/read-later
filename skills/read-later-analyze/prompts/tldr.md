# TL;DR and key claims

`tldr`: what the piece says, in 2 to 4 short sentences and at most 80 words, for a reader deciding from these sentences alone whether to open it. State the actual claim or finding, not the topic. "Argues that code review should gate on tests, not on reading diffs" is a TL;DR; "Discusses code review in the age of AI" is not.

`keyClaims`: 1 to 3 short statements the author asserts, each one checkable. Prefer the specific and surprising over the general. Keep the author's numbers.

Rules:
- Report the author's meaning, not their marketing. If the title overclaims, the TL;DR does not.
- Neutral register. No praise, no "this insightful piece".
- Quote at most one short phrase.
- If the text is not an article (a landing page, a login wall, a listing, an error page), say so in the TL;DR and set `contentType` to `not-an-article`.
