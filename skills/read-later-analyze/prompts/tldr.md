# TL;DR

Write `tldr`: what the piece says, in 2 to 4 sentences, for a reader who will decide from these sentences alone whether to open it. State the actual claim or finding, not the topic. "Argues that code review should gate on tests, not on reading diffs" is a TL;DR; "Discusses code review in the age of AI" is not.

Then `keyClaims`: 2 to 5 short statements the author asserts, each one checkable. Prefer the specific and surprising over the general. Include numbers when the author gives them.

Rules:
- Use the author's meaning, not their marketing. If the title overclaims, the TL;DR does not.
- No praise, no "this insightful article". Neutral register.
- Do not quote more than one short phrase.
- If the text is not an article (a landing page, a login wall, a listing), say so in the TL;DR and set `contentType` accordingly.
