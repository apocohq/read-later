# Scores

Two integer scores, 0 to 10, each with `reason`: one sentence naming the evidence in the text that decided the number. Score what the text supports, not what the title promises. Both are about the article itself; they do not depend on who reads it.

`hardWon`: how much of the piece comes from real building, data or experience that cost effort to obtain.
- 0: restates what everyone says; no first-hand detail.
- 5: some first-hand detail or data, mixed with general advice.
- 10: specific experience, failures, trade-offs, real numbers or mechanisms from someone close to the work.

`grounded`: how much the piece rests on specific, supported claims rather than excitement or selling.
- 0: runs on urgency, popularity or fear of missing out, or exists to sell a product, service or the author; claims are broad and unsupported.
- 5: makes real claims with thin support; some promotion or padding.
- 10: specific claims, supported by data, examples or a clear chain of reasoning; admits limits; no sales agenda.

Be strict. Most web writing sits between 3 and 6 on both. Reserve 9 and 10 for pieces you would cite.

For a video or podcast you only see the publisher's description. Score what it shows: who speaks and from what experience (hard-won), and whether it names concrete topics and claims or just sells the episode (grounded). Do not assume depth the description does not show.
